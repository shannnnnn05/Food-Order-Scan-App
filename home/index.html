Check a food page before you pay.

LigtasPangan reads the public content and payment form of online food ordering pages. It looks for fake sellers, phishing payment forms, hidden fees and copied reviews, then shows a clear warning before any money leaves your hands.

Install in your browser

Manifest V3

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers take the same folder.

01
Unzip the project

Extract the package anywhere you can keep it, for example inside your Documents folder. Do not delete the folder after installing.

02
Open the extensions page

Chrome: chrome://extensions
Edge: edge://extensions

03
Load the extension folder

Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, then select the extension folder and confirm.

After loading
  • The install guide opens automatically. Keep it open until you finish setup.
  • Pin the LigtasPangan icon from the puzzle piece menu.
  • Open a food ordering page. A badge appears at the bottom right.
  • Click the badge or the toolbar icon to see the score and the reasons.
Requirements
  • Chrome 109, Edge 109 or any recent Chromium browser.
  • No npm install needed for the extension.
  • Node.js 18 or newer only if you want to run the practice pages.
  • No account and no internet connection needed for scanning.
  • A Gmail address is only used as a label when the browser is already signed in.

What it looks for

Each rule adds weight to a risk score from 0 to 100. Every point is explained in plain language.

01 · Seller

Fake or missing identity

No business permit, no physical address, no contact number, or a page that hides who is selling.

02 · Payment

Phishing payment forms

Card, password or one-time PIN fields, insecure form actions and forms that post to another site.

03 · Price

Price traps

A checkout total that is higher than the advertised price, or fees that appear only at the last step.

04 · Reviews

Copied reviews

Repeated wording and bursts of perfect ratings posted within a short window.

05 · Domain

Risky addresses

No HTTPS, raw IP addresses, punycode brand imitation, hyphen-heavy hosts and high-abuse domains.

06 · Payment demand

Advance payment pressure

Full payment required before verification, especially when no recognized gateway is used.

Privacy

  • Page content is analysed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Card numbers, PINs and passwords are never read. Only field names are checked.
  • No account and no sign in are needed. No tracking and no analytics.
  • Only a Gmail address from an already signed in browser is used, as the name beside each check. Without one the check is saved as a guest.
  • Reports stay in your browser and can be cleared any time.
  • The extension asks for storage, tabs and page access on http and https pages.
  • It does not read email, private messages or other tabs.
  • It never processes payments and never stores card data.
  • A safe result is not a guarantee. Always check the permit, reviews and receipt.

Questions

Why are all records zero?

The project has not been used by real sellers or reviewers yet. Zero is the honest starting point.

Does the extension need the local server?

No. The server only serves the practice pages. Scanning works on any website without it.

Will it warn me on every website?

No. Pages that do not look like an online seller with a payment form are skipped, so the badge stays hidden.

Can I trust a safe result with money?

Treat it as one signal. Pay through a recognized gateway and keep the receipt. The tool warns, it does not insure.
