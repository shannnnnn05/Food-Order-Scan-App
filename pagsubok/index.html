Project home
Test environment

Compare the scanner result with the expected badge

Start the local server from the project folder with node serve/server.js, then open each page below. A badge appears at the bottom right of every page that the scanner checks.

Pages

01 Tunay na Tindahan Complete seller details, a business permit and a recognized payment gateway. Safe 02 Pekeng Kusina Unknown seller, card form, higher checkout total and copied reviews. Unsafe 03 Pekeng Bayaran Card, password and PIN form that posts to another site. Unsafe 04 Presyong Panlinlang Checkout total is higher than the advertised price. Caution 05 Pekeng Rebyu Copied five-star reviews posted in one burst. Caution 06 Pekeng Kantina Copies a school stall and asks for an advance transfer. Unsafe

What you should see

Safe result. A green confirmation appears at the top of the page. The badge shows a low score and stays at the bottom right.

Cautious or unsafe result. A red bar appears first with the message about the page. Press OK to open the alert page, which lists every reason and the steps to take.

No result. Pages that do not look like an online seller are skipped, so the badge stays hidden.

What is checked

Seller identity, payment form, price totals, review text, connection security and domain patterns. Every result shows the reasons behind the score.

These pages are inert. No payment is taken and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Before testing

1. Load the extension folder in your browser.
2. Run node serve/server.js from the project folder.
3. Open http://127.0.0.1:4173/pagsubok/ and pick a page.
4. Open the toolbar popup to see the score and the reasons.
