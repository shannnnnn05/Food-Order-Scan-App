How a check runs

01
Reads public signals

Menu prices, contact details, business permit, payment fields and review text are inspected on your device.

02
Scores the page

Weighted rules produce a risk score from 0 to 100 and a list of reasons for the result.

03
Warns before payment

Safe pages show a short confirmation. Unsafe pages show a warning bar first, then the full alert page.

Privacy

The scanner is built to look at pages, not people.

  • Page content is analysed on your device. No page content leaves the browser.
  • Card numbers, one-time PINs and passwords are detected by field name only. Their values are never read, copied or stored.
  • Reports are saved in this browser only. They are not uploaded to a server.
  • Checks are saved in this browser as a log, under the Gmail address of the signed in browser or a guest name when there is none.
  • No account creation, no sign in form, no analytics and no tracking identifiers are used.
  • Only the storage, tabs and identity permissions are requested, plus page access needed to scan food ordering sites.
  • Seller lists are stored with chrome.storage.sync and follow your browser profile.

What the scanner does not do

  • It does not process payments.
  • It does not read private messages, email or other tabs.
  • It does not guarantee that a seller is honest. It reports visible warning signs only.
  • It does not replace checking reviews, permits and receipts yourself.
