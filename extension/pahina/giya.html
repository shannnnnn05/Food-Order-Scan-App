Browser extension for food ordering safety

Know if a food page is safe before you pay.

LigtasPangan checks the public content and payment form of online food ordering pages. It looks for fake sellers, phishing payment forms, price traps and fabricated reviews, then warns you in plain language.

The scanner is already active in this browser. Open any food ordering page and the result appears automatically.

What you will see

Safe page

This is a safe and good food ordering app

A green message confirms the page passed the check. You can order normally, and the badge stays at the bottom right with the risk score.

Unsafe page

This Page is not safe, see it for yourself. Just click the OK to proceed

A red warning bar appears at the top of the page first. Press OK to open the full alert page, which lists every reason the page was flagged and what you should do next.

Installation

01
Open your browser extensions page

Chrome: go to chrome://extensions. Edge: go to edge://extensions.

02
Load the extension folder

Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, then select the extension folder from this project.

03
Pin the icon

Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin LigtasPangan. Open a food ordering page to see the badge and the result.

If this page opened by itself, the installation already finished. To test the scanner, start the local test server from the project folder with node serve/server.js, then open http://127.0.0.1:4173/pagsubok/.

What the scanner checks

Seller identity

Business permit, physical address, contact number and about or contact pages.

Payment safety

Card and PIN fields, insecure form actions, cross-site form targets and known payment gateways.

Price honesty

Menu price against checkout price, plus fees that appear only at the last step.

Review quality

Repeated wording and bursts of perfect reviews posted within a short time.

Domain risk

Missing HTTPS, raw IP addresses, punycode brand imitation, hyphens and high-abuse domains.

Advance payment

Full payment demands without a recognized gateway or verifiable seller details.

Permissions

storage
Saves your settings, trusted and blocked seller lists, the reports you submit and the log of every check.
tabs
Reads the address of the page you are on so the badge and popup can show its result.
identity
Reads the Gmail address already signed in to the browser, used only as the name beside each saved check. When there is none, the check is saved as a guest.
Page access on http and https
Required to read public page content and detect payment fields. Text field values are never read.

Questions

Does it need a server to work?

No. The scanner runs fully inside the extension. The local server is only for the practice pages.

Does it read what I type?

No. It checks field names to detect card or PIN inputs. Values, passwords and messages are never read or stored.

Why are all records zero?

This is a new project. There are no ratings, reviews, verified sellers or alerts yet, and nothing is seeded to look busy.

Can it guarantee a seller is honest?

No. It reports visible warning signs. Always check the permit, reviews and receipt yourself.
